Car Maintenance Tips

Regular Oil Changes

Keep your engine running smoothly by changing oil every 3,000-5,000 miles. Fresh oil reduces friction and extends engine life.

Tire Rotation

Rotate your tires every 6,000-8,000 miles to ensure even wear and maximize tire lifespan.

Battery Maintenance

Check your battery regularly and clean any corrosion on the terminals to ensure reliable starts.
